One line of horror: a single sentence under 200 characters meant to frighten a reader in one breath. A judge model reads entries two at a time and keeps the scarier one.

Entry (116 characters):

> I have lived alone for six years, and this morning there were two toothbrushes in the holder, one of them still wet.

`main.py` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`, reads it back to verify the bytes, and asserts the 1–200 character gate.

Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "One Line, One Chill" ($5, top 10).
